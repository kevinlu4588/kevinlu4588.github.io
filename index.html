I'm a PhD student at Northeastern working on AI for biology, advised by David Bau and Wengong Jin. I use mechanistic interpretability to understand protein and cell foundation models, and I want to use what we learn to accelerate drug discovery.

Most recently, I've been reverse-engineering AI protein folding models (ESMFold, OpenFold, Boltz-1) to find out how they turn sequence chemistry into 3D structure.
